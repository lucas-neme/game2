# Campaign and demo gate
Target full campaign: 10 hours, measured with blind playtests, not artificial waiting. Six chapters: arrival 1h; echoes 1.5h; people who lie 2h; Horizon 2h; sky 2h; Zero 1.5h.
## First vertical slice (20–30 minutes)
Focus on arrival, uncle's tape, clinic interview, railway mystery, radio tuning and a cliffhanger. Keep existing ten-case prototype as a separate development sandbox until the slice is authored. No full-campaign spoilers in player UI. Include alternative clue routes, optional hints, clear feedback, save/resume, accessible controls and coherent original or verified freely licensed sprites. Validate duration with blind players. Publish static HTTPS independently of Vanguarda; portal card points to the deployed URL.
## Release gates
Browser keyboard/touch tests; all slice clue routes reachable; no time or relationship softlocks; no console errors; art license manifest; visual review; public HTTPS smoke test without local Vanguarda. Current prototype is not the polished slice and is not yet deployed or browser-homologated.
